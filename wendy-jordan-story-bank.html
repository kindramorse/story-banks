01The doctor who told me to exerciseREADY · YOUR LANE

You went in about an injury and left believing something about your body. The most on-brand story you own, because your reader has had this exact appointment.

You mentioned you already made a reel about this one. That means the raw version is out there and this can be the fuller telling, or a second angle on it.

Open in the exam room. Dwell on the body. Land on the scale, not on a lesson.

Hook options
  • I went to the doctor about a fall and left thinking about my body instead. context
  • I'd been doing HIIT workouts for eight months when he told me I should exercise. contrarian
  • He weighed me first. Then he said it. intrigue
I slipped on the ice and ended up with a hematoma so big I went in to get it looked at. They weighed me first. It was the most I had ever weighed in my life, and I remember sitting there thinking, I promised myself I would never let it get above this number. Then the doctor came in. I asked him a simple question. What can I do to make sure this heals properly? And he said, you need to be active. You need to exercise. I had been doing HIIT workouts for eight months. He didn't ask. He looked at me and decided. I don't remember if I said anything back. I think I just said okay. What I remember is my whole body going hot and clammy, and wanting to crawl into a hole, and wanting to cover myself up and get out of that room. I went in about a bruise. I left thinking he must look at me and see someone who doesn't try.
CTA · three clean beatsYou can be doing everything right and still get treated like you're not. If you've ever left an appointment feeling smaller than when you walked in, I see you. Follow along. I'm putting words to this all week.

The proof is that you HAD been working out for eight months. Keep that. It's the whole reason the story stings and the whole reason your reader trusts you. Do not add a lesson after "someone who doesn't try." End there.

Here is a partial list of things I tried before this. Metabolism pills. Hydroxycut. Shakes, more than one kind. Beachbody. Meal plans. Workout plans. Weight Watchers. A cabinet full of vitamins and supplements. Every plan built for someone who eats meat, which I don't. So when I finally decided to try something new, I did the research. I read everything. And logically, I knew it should work. And underneath that, I thought: it'll work for everybody else. It won't work for me. I want to tell you what I actually did with that thought. I lowered my goal. On purpose. I picked a number I thought I could reach so I wouldn't be disappointed when I stalled out. That is what years of nothing working does to a person. Seventy seven pounds later, I bought a pair of size 8 jeans at Old Navy, and I stood in the fitting room thinking they must be mislabeled. I went and tried on a size 10 at a thrift store from a brand I'd never heard of, just to check. It fit too.
CTA · three clean beatsWhen nothing has worked for years, you stop being able to hope. That's not a character flaw, it's evidence. If you've quietly lowered what you're hoping for so it hurts less when it doesn't happen, this one's for you. Follow along.

"I lowered my goal on purpose so I wouldn't be disappointed" is the most human line in this bank. It is the whole story. Do not cut it to save space. And notice the post lands on checking a second pair of jeans, not on the number. Keep it that way.

Belief post · short, text on screen

For a day you want something quick. One belief, no arc.

There's a specific kind of tired that comes from trying things. Not from doing nothing. From doing everything, over and over, and having none of it hold. After enough of that, you don't stop wanting it. You just stop expecting it. You lower the number. You hedge. You say you'll be happy with less so it hurts less when less is all you get. I did that. If that's where you are, you are not lazy and you are not undisciplined. You're just someone who has been let down a lot.
CTA · three clean beatsYou didn't stop wanting it. You stopped expecting it. If you've been let down by every plan you've tried, that's information about the plans. Follow along.
