How to read this

Each story is built the same way: your actual words from the call, then the moment underneath, then drafts you can post. The drafts follow one law. Character can't be claimed, only revealed. So none of them announce who you are. They stage a moment and let the reader arrive at the conclusion themselves. That is on purpose. The instant a post explains itself, it hands the discovery away, and the discovery was the whole point.

Delivery is what you already know works for you: a 7 second B-roll clip, the hook on screen, the story in the caption. No talking head required.

ReadyTied straight to your lane. Clean enough to post once you have read the words and they sound like you. Start here.
PersonalPowerful, true, and tender. Yours to sit with. Post only if and when you want to, and only after you have read the exact words.
HeldCaptured so you can see it exists, deliberately not drafted. Needs distance, or someone else's blessing, or both.
