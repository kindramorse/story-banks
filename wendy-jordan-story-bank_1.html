Story Bank · From one excavation session

Wendy Jordan The stories your ideal client is living right now, and doesn't have words for yet.

Every story below came out of one session. You lived all of it. None of it is content until you decide it is, and you are the only one who would ever post it. Read these as options, not a to-do list. Each draft is a starting point in your voice, meant to be rewritten freely until it sounds like you talking.

How to read this

Each story is tagged for readiness. Open the ones that pull at you. Inside you will find your own quoted words from the session (the accuracy anchor, so you can check nothing was invented), then one or more post formats with hook options, a full draft, a three beat call to action, and a note on what to protect.

The rule underneath all of it: character can't be claimed, only revealed. These posts stage a moment and get out of the way, so your reader reaches the conclusion herself. That conclusion is trust.

What changed in this version

This revision applies three updates from the refreshed build protocol and operating manual, and adds one new post.

1. The work you want more of. A new rule says content is a filter, not a net: only make posts about the work you want more of. Story 10 was reframed so it attracts clients who want to be truly seen, not the most draining custom cases, and a new Story 12 points at the in-person retreat direction you said you are building toward.

2. Cleaner landings. The marquee carousels (01, 02, 03) no longer stack two closing beats. Each now ends on a single landing, or the one sanctioned two-beat move: a reader turn, then a mirror reflection.

3. A sharper image. Where it fits her photo stories, a post now uses the go-forward line, you cannot see the picture when you are the one inside the frame.
